Research & Publications

Real-Time Adaptive Underwater Visual Restoration Using HSV-LAB Environmental Profiling on Low-Power Devices

IEEE SPICES 2026 – Intelligent Computing Track (Paper ID: 1810) | Oct 2026 | Ongoing

Co-author, Dept. of CSE, SRM University AP

Adaptive, training-free underwater video enhancement for AUVs and low-power devices where GPUs are unavailable and a single fixed enhancement pipeline cannot handle changing water conditions.

  • Environmental profiling: converts each 720×360 frame to HSV and CIE L*a*b*, extracts 4 optical statistics (mean Hue, Saturation, Value and std. of L*) and scores it against 9 optical environments (clear ocean, deep sea, coral reef, turbid coastal, muddy, polluted, shallow coastal, tropical blue, artificial light) using heuristic confidence scoring, with no training data or neural network
  • Confidence-aware pipeline selection: runs a single optimized pipeline on clear decisions; when the top two environments are close (|c1 − c2| < 0.3, c2 > 0.4) it runs both and blends outputs by confidence (w1F1 + w2F2) to avoid abrupt transitions; low-confidence frames fall back to a generic White Balance → CLAHE → Gamma chain
  • Environment-specific enhancement: pipelines composed from 12 primitives in 5 groups (luminance/contrast, chromatic restoration, dehazing, structural enhancement, noise suppression), e.g. Deep Sea: Gamma → Retinex MSR → Bilateral → CLAHE; Polluted: Bilateral → Dehaze → CLAHE → Laplacian
  • Temporal stability & quality gate: a 10-frame sliding-window brightness stabilizer (β = 0.5) removes flicker; a Contrast Improvement Index gate (σenh/σorig ≥ 1.05) accepts only frames that genuinely improve, preventing artifacts on already-clean frames
  • Results: 30 FPS on CPU with no GPU; averages of UIQM 190.20, UCIQE 53.44, Entropy 4.32 and CII 1.27 across all 9 environments, with peak contrast gain on coral reef (CII 1.83); ablation study confirmed each module contributes a distinct improvement
  • Packaged as the Underwater Vision Pro desktop app (live camera + batch video, per-frame metric export)

Technologies used: Python, OpenCV, NumPy
